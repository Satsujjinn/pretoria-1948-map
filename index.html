Web Tools for Maps, Photos, and Drawings

Drag 4 corner pins to flatten angled photos of blueprints and sketches into straight, rectangular documents.
Scale Model & Cut-List Studio Models
Convert real-world measurements into scale model cut sizes, subtract board thickness for clean corners, and measure model photos.

Enlarge photos up to 4x, sharpen blurry edges, and auto-balance brightness and contrast.

Align and blend two overlapping aerial map strips while keeping side instrument dials intact.
